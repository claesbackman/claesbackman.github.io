Notes on the digital edition

The text, tables and figure captions are generated from the LaTeX source of the working paper with pandoc. Tables show the paper's numbers. Figures 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and A5 are drawn from the estimate tables that the Stata pipeline writes, so each can be switched to a table and its numbers downloaded. Figure 4 and Appendix Figures A1 to A4 are the paper's static images. Known differences between the website and the paper versions of a figure:

  • Figure 6 draws the fitted line from the paper's slope and the points' means; the paper's version also shades the country-bootstrap 95 percent band, which the website omits. The slope, HC2 standard error and wild p-value are printed on the chart.
  • Figure 7 is the paper's figure; the website's tooltip additionally shows each country's benchmark range.
  • Figure 8 shows seven of the ten rows of Appendix Table A7, the same seven as the paper's figure. The hedge-by-volatility interactions and the linear wealth slope are in the table.
  • Appendix Figure A5 adds the fitted κ line's intercept from the plotted points; the paper's version anchors the κ = 0 and κ = 1 lines at the origin.

Software

Estimates: Stata 18.5 with reghdfe, boottest, estout and coefplot. Collection: Python 3 and the OpenRouter API. Site: pandoc 3.8, Python 3, D3 7.9, KaTeX 0.16.
