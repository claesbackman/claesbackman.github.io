Robustness explorer

Every result in the paper is one point in a grid of specifications. This page lets you walk the grid. Pick an outcome, choose any combination of policy index, controls, fixed effects, sample, weights and dependent-variable definition, and see the full impulse response with equivalence tests. The specification curve below shows every estimated combination at once, and the remaining panels show leave-one-out estimates, alternative inference and the event-study variants around the five large reforms.

All estimates were produced by the Stata scripts in ProjectCode/4_*.do using the same local projection as the paper (Equation 1), with standard errors clustered by country. The baseline specification in the paper is the overall MaPPED index with the Eurostat control set, country and year fixed effects, the full sample, no weights and the cumulative dependent variable. Figures on the paper pages link here with the matching specification preselected.

4. Alternative inference

With 28 country clusters, asymptotic clustered standard errors may be unreliable. The table compares them with heteroskedasticity-robust, two-way clustered and Driscoll–Kraay standard errors, with wild cluster bootstrap p-values and confidence intervals, and with randomization inference.
