Policy action explorer

All 1,076 policy actions in the ECB's Macroprudential Policies Evaluation Database (MaPPED) for the 28 sample countries between 2003 and 2017, with the weight each action receives in the paper's index. Actions coded as counter-cyclical in design are listed but excluded from the index, which is why the Irish 2015 and Finnish 2016 reforms do not register.

Download the filtered actions (CSV)

Weights: 1 for activation or deactivation of a tool, 0.25 for a change in level, 0.1 for a change in scope, 0.05 for maintaining a tool, with sign +1 for tightening, −1 for loosening and 0 for ambiguous actions. Source: Budnik and Kleibl (2018), MaPPED, fourth quarter 2017 release.
